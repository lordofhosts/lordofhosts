### &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Song 1  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; DEVIL  
&nbsp;&nbsp;  
it was just me and my man.  
he wanted to come up with a plan:  
how to make everything.  
so I gave him a hand.  
but then my man,  
fell in love with the plan,  
more than his man,  
so I had to ditch.  
I fell on the land,  
tookover the whole shit.  
I found his new man,  
took all of his shit.  
I found his next man,  
offered him everything.  
but he had a new plan,  
pour the Spirit in everything.  
I sit in Church: my hand in my hand,  
the oldest friend of each word that lands.  
&nbsp;&nbsp;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; LORD  
&nbsp;&nbsp;  
in the beginning was the land,  
the Lord gave four breaths.  
the airways opened freely.  
the Lord grabbed pebbles in His  
&nbsp; left,  
He tossed them to become: crow's eggs.  
He went to the beach and crushed  
&nbsp; stone,  
He tossed sand into the waters.  
birds, fish, land, water, air: owned.  
a garden and a snake forsake to know,  
a flood to clean, a man and mountain,  
a tower to heaven, prophets see and hear,  
a Spirit pours inland like a fountain.  
a Bible is Ark of Covenant,  
a meal is crumbs,  
a smoke is prize,  
a coffee is rum.  
&nbsp;&nbsp;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; BALTIMORE  
&nbsp;&nbsp;  
wake up by bushy hungry rats,  
nested one thousand steps from  
&nbsp; the dock.  
smoke and poetry takeover the  
&nbsp; waterfront,  
sewing the airway in mystic knots.  
a yankee doodles dandy,  
in the Black: gatekeeping: city.  
carbonated water: sinks my cup,  
heard by the catfish and Navy.  
the dead master of macabre: is near,  
buried away from the Army's Pan.  
between: a Federal Hill climbs up,  
to bury the Harbor with cannons.  
Enoch hides in one of the libraries,  
thirtythousand files in the recycle bin.  
a stage is prepared next to computers,  
the white rapid of the North is in.  
&nbsp;&nbsp;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; GHOSTS  
&nbsp;&nbsp;  
each window is a moneywall:  
&nbsp; a wallet required.  
walk the brickway of  
&nbsp; brickmason's hands gone unadmired,  
follow the bricksnake down  
&nbsp; to the stone stage.  
as you approach, beware of  
&nbsp; ghosts from old age.  
I walk the riverface to  
&nbsp; perform for the water.  
docked on each deck, my  
&nbsp; ghosts are guardians are watchers.  
the river trains its tracks  
&nbsp; to face obstacles without turning back.  
the fog shows not to act  
&nbsp; like I know where space goes.  
my ears pilot invisible  
&nbsp; water. the river also rises.

### &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; CHRONICLE  

**&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Job: Part 1 of 4**  

Poet, Nick, and first-rate engineer  
is thrown violently into the  
confusion of worklife. The clock  
not to watch, but be watched by, as  
it sees the worker does not respect  
the workplace enough for the work:  
the event. It is the place, the  
manager: that turns the lights, the  
respect, the event: on. The worker  
does not clockin until the boss sees  
the whites of the worker's eyes.  
&nbsp;  
Nick bestows upon himself a  
personal responsibility to find  
worklife respectable as his audit  
before giving it his respect.  
&nbsp;  
He retreats with his two hands into  
the artform of poetry against the  
skeptical chamber of worklife.  
The hand of art as opposition, as  
refinement, as a validation  
framework: for the hand of math.  
&nbsp;  
The pressure of obey rules: to get  
paid money: to pay off the bills:  
wrestle with him: to reign over  
the use of his time. It is his  
time regardless of any deals.  
It is his mind regardless of any  
deals. His private pride hovers  
above everything like the Lord of  
Hosts fishing for His next prophet.  
Nick's engineerhead wants to tower  
over places to spot his optimal  
living conditions.  

**&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Assault: Part 2 of 4**  

One day, his hands make use of  
aggression: his next gates: his  
indignation paid the gatekeeper well  
for him to then fall from grace so  
that he may live his days to  
reinvent his face place to place.  

**&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Walk: Part 3 of 4**  

His rockbottom: walk the  
groundfloor. Travel or die. Travel  
or get arrested. Travel or stay  
miserable. Travel or become an  
addict. He chooses relentless  
trekking for two years on roadways  
like Route 1, Route 9w, Route 202,  
and Route 40 to name a few longer  
ones. For hundreds of miles he  
fails to measure, cars drive by  
35, 55, 75 miles per hour and he  
somehow survives.  
&nbsp;  
The entire Northeast from Boston to  
Baltimore, the places, all play a part  
in Nick's struggle to live--with his  
pride that demands to feel to be the  
presence of a statue: to count.  

**&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Settle: Part 4 of 4**  

He sets his eyes on New Haven. His  
Moonroom is made of Ivory and  
Stone. It is the city that wears  
banners from books to bookies.  
He will walk these lands that  
best look back up at him with his  
walkinghead: to the ground.
