           Small 
           A SKETCH 

feel small to be with Her, 
feel big to socialize. 
feel small to go inner, 
feel big to hypnotize. 
feel small to be a cuddler, 
feel big to optimize. 
walking balloon head, 
getting paid by tread.

           Saturday 
           A SONG 

the long roads are my home, 
loose cigarette lottery hunt. 
coffee I hand to myself, 
eye a mart for brunch. 
a cart from the ground is a blast, 
taste to headspace rush. 
my knapsack hugs my butt, 
carries my day's lush. 
fermented tea stings my mouth, 
kisses the alcoholic inside. 
my luggage is my witness, 
I walk a path of demand to supplied. 
lounge at the park, 
afternoon, dawn, dusk, dark. 
my hand swindles my heart, 
with ink from insideout art.

           Obalisk 
           A SONG 

my root hugs a tree, 
curled up in a cocoon. 
a couple cars twentyfive by, 
one way below my perched mood. 
four before dawn my numb toes touch street, 
Pisces in my feet fish for looseys. 
bricks in my hand pick up bud, 
the frozen snowglobe finds bone easily. 
smoke from the little blue rooster, 
in Washington Park that walls off South. 
the cops doublepoach the hallway, 
drinking Monsters as a Camel in my mouth. 
I'm a walking cold stone obalisk, 
my tattoos minting a bloody ruby past. 
my chest's pulse thermometer hibernates, 
winter's cocaine body armor falls into me fast.
