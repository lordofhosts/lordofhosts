           WALNUT 
           A BOOK FOR SETTLEMENT 

One man's personal responsibility 
to managing his own mind by his 
sole hand has him make 
lifechanging choices that make 
travel his last and only resort. 
Travel or die. Travel or get 
arrested. Travel or stay miserable. 
Travel or addiction on Mass and 
Cass in Boston. He chooses 
relentless trekking roadways like 
Route 1, Route 9w, and Route 202 
to name a few longer ones. His 
lifestyle is documented by many 
places in the eight states from 
Boston to Baltimore. He chooses 
to take a hint from the next 
place and stay put in Norwalk 
Connecticut. The place is a 
large town and city parts. It 
reminds him of his hometown 
signature Merrimack Valley. He 
knows how to live on the move 
but now he tells the tale of 
settling down after such a 
strong journey in mental health.

           Wind 

The windways roll between clouds 
like bowling balls in lanes. I 
hear the smacking of the wind 
thud to the eardrum and airdrum 
above. 

Above is the only direction. It 
is the direction of space. 

Death is below. It is the 
concrete so many layers dogpile 
until there is no space.

           Walnut 

I tell people my line of work is 
street art. I do a character. I 
call myself Walnut. 

Walnut started as a voice. I picked 
it up after swans visited me by the 
waterfront outside of New Haven. 

By the time my right to travel 
walks the Northeast from Boston to 
Baltimore, I'm finding the oldest 
Theatre in America is in Philly 
called Walnut.
